The Bourgoyne and Young model (BYM) is a widely used method for predicting the rate of penetration (ROP) in drilling operations, relying on eight unknown parameters (coefficients) that depend on the specific ground formation. While the original authors proposed using multiple regression to determine these coefficients, this method can sometimes yield physically meaningless results. In this research, a Genetic Algorithm (GA) is employed to determine these coefficients. By applying GA to sensory data collected from nine wells in the Khangiran Iranian gas field, the study demonstrates that this approach provides more accurate ROP predictions and produces physically meaningful coefficients compared to conventional regression methods.
